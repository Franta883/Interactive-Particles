# Satisfying-game

Interactive Particles is a calming, browser-based particle field. Gray dots sit connected by faint lines, resting quietly until you click or drag across the canvas. Each click fires a shockwave that pushes nearby particles outward, and dragging leaves a trail of smaller waves plus a soft glowing light that follows your pointer. Particles always spring back to their original spots, so the scene resets itself.

A collapsible control panel lets you fine-tune the experience: shockwave size, recovery speed, wave strength, network strength (lower = bigger push), particle density, and glow size/intensity. Press H to hide the panel and enjoy the field on its own.
